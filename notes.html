The Project: In-House Camouflage Armory

Background

Written in 2026.

During my military service I was a combat soldier in an intelligence collection unit, responsible for camouflaging ambush positions for missions. As the unit's armory officer, I saw the need to produce camouflage equipment and positions in-house: to save costs, to build professional expertise, to work at a level that never compromises on detail in any mission, and to adapt to every new operational need.

Planning & Execution

I led a steering forum of the unit's logistics and combat officers, built a work Gantt chart, secured resources (funding, logistics, procurement, training for other soldiers, technological capabilities) and got the plans approved by everyone involved.

The Outcome

In-House Production

A professional armory room holding everything needed for end-to-end in-house production, ambush positions and camouflage capabilities tailored to each required mission, and those capabilities passed on to the other soldiers in the company.

Key Competencies

  • Project planning: a Gantt chart and a structured work plan.
  • Leadership and stakeholder management: leading a forum and getting plans approved by everyone involved.
  • Resource management: funding, procurement and logistics.
  • Cost awareness: in-house production to save costs.
  • Attention to detail: no compromise on any mission.
  • Adaptability: tailoring capabilities to each new operational need.
  • Training and knowledge transfer: teaching other soldiers.
